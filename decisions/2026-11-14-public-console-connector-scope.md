# Public planning scope: Console and Node Connector

Date: 2026-11-14

## Decision

The `mcpdev80/baseharbor-console` and `mcpdev80/baseharbor-node-connector` repositories remain public and are archived as historical snapshots. Their README files and repository descriptions identify the archived status. No further releases or updates are planned for these repositories.

All open BaseHarbor-plan task cards dedicated to Console delivery or Node Connector delivery are closed with resolution `dropped`. The Console product epic is closed. There is no separate Node Connector epic in the current planning vault; its remaining top-level delivery item is the `nc-3` task card.

This closes future work in the public planning scope. It does not remove repository history, tags, releases, previously delivered code, accepted evidence, or historical planning records.

## Boundaries

This decision covers repository lifecycle and direct Console/Node Connector planning cards. It does not alter historical acceptance records or determine unrelated Core, Demo, Grafana, or release-plan scope.
