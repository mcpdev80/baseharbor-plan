## Contract review

The candidate adds an application-class log writer and tests registry reconciliation, policy
disable, and preservation of a reference-class source. However, it collapses service identity.
The existing `docs/architecture/runtime-log-source-contract.md` defines normalized source identity
by application, environment, class, provider, and service. `Execution.ProvisionAndBind` invokes
`Driver.Bind` for each planned binding, and `Driver.Bind` passes its concrete `binding.Logs.Service`.
The new `applicationLogSource` nevertheless puts the constant `workload` in every source ID.
`ReconcileMatching` replaces all application-class log rows in the whole app/environment boundary
with the one desired row. A second service therefore overwrites the first service's target. The
render-time writer also writes target service `workload` instead of actual service names.

The regression currently named `TestApplicationClassLogSourceDisabledPolicyReclaims` deliberately
retargets `api` to `worker` and asserts that only one signal remains. That contradicts the
documented service identity and leaves multi-service workloads without a source for one or more
streams.

**CODEX-REVIEW: REQUEST CHANGES.** Do not merge this head.

Recipe: preserve service identity in the source ID and reconcile boundary. Have render-time
reconciliation register the actual active service set and remove only services no longer desired;
each Loki `Bind` must retain its service entry. Add a two-service regression proving both IDs and
targets survive repeated render and Bind reconciliation and pruning one removed service leaves the
other intact. Keep the same-ID/different-class/provider protection. Do not broaden into the
#1691 environment-filter behavior. Re-run exact-head required CI after correction.
